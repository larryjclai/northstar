## Design Context

### Users
northstar is for individual investors in Taiwan who want a calm, private, native way to track stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, cash positions, dividends, and long-term wealth progress across Apple devices. They are long-term investors, salary earners, and self-directed planners, not day traders living inside broker terminals.

### Brand Personality
Calm, trustworthy, directional. The product should feel like a quiet navigation instrument for wealth: precise enough for real investment decisions, warm enough to revisit often, and opinionated enough to keep users focused on long-term progress.

### Aesthetic Direction
Primary mood is a refined dark-mode Apple-native finance interface inspired by Copilot Money's approachable financial clarity and Robinhood's lightweight native momentum. The interface should use a deep, quiet canvas, bright semantic data colors, chart-first summaries, tabular financial typography, and progressive disclosure. It should avoid broker-terminal density, spreadsheet trackers, crypto neon, generic navy-and-gold fintech, promotional bank-app clutter, and interfaces that monetize attention.

### Design Principles
1. Make wealth directional: every major screen should clarify whether the user is moving toward their long-term goal.
2. Let data carry the color: keep the app frame quiet and reserve vivid color for growth, risk, warning, selection, and chart meaning.
3. Stage complexity progressively: start with a legible summary, then reveal holdings, events, and transaction details on demand.
4. Earn trust before delight: calculations, timestamps, currencies, price freshness, and portfolio events must be explainable.
5. Stay native and private: use SwiftUI, Dynamic Type, accessibility-aware contrast, local-first language, and platform-appropriate motion.
